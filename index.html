About

Born in Kandy in 1994, Dhanith Sri makes music that moves between alternative rock, R&B and rap.

He broke through in 2018 with “Pandama”, where high vocals ride over traditional Sri Lankan drum patterns and synthesizers, and followed it with songs including “Sandaganawa” and “Amme”.

His 2023 album Raplanthaya earned two Derana Music Video Awards in 2025. That same year he staged Alokawarsha — The Tale of Limitless Minds, a full live concert under rings of light.
